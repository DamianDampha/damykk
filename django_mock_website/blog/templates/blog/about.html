About Gambia Weather Tracker

Our Mission

Gambia Weather Tracker is dedicated to providing accurate, real-time weather information for The Gambia, a beautiful West African country. We aim to help residents, travelers, and weather enthusiasts stay informed about current conditions and forecasts.

About The Gambia

The Gambia is a small West African nation located along the Atlantic coast. It's known for its:

  • Tropical climate with distinct dry and rainy seasons
  • Beautiful beaches and coastal landscapes
  • River Gambia, one of Africa's most important waterways
  • Diverse wildlife and natural reserves
  • Rich cultural heritage and vibrant communities

Weather Information

We track weather data for major cities including:

  • Banjul - The capital city
  • Serekunda - Largest city and commercial hub
  • Bakau - Coastal resort area
  • Farafenni - Northern city
  • Kaur - Eastern region

Climate Seasons

Dry Season (November - May): Cool temperatures, minimal rainfall, perfect for tourism

Rainy Season (June - October): Warm temperatures, frequent rainfall, lush landscapes

How We Work

Our platform aggregates weather data and presents it in an easy-to-understand format. We provide:

  • Current weather conditions for each city
  • 7-day weather forecasts
  • Severe weather alerts
  • Historical weather patterns
